# Foley source licenses

All third-party audio in this directory is offered under **CC0 1.0 Universal**.
The source and every byte checksum are recorded in `manifest.json`. The audio
is downloaded from the creator's original published URL, not a mirror.

- **Kenney — Impact Sounds 1.0**: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds . The
  original package contains the creator's license, retained verbatim in
  `LICENSE-Kenney.txt`. It explicitly permits personal, educational and
  commercial use and states that credit is not mandatory. Only the 20 listed
  audio members are extracted; the checked source ZIP is retained.
- **Thimras — Battle at sea**: https://opengameart.org/content/battle-at-sea .
  The creator's publication assigns CC0 to the naval sound effects. These are
  authored game sounds, not a claim of field-recorded historical artillery.
- **RandomMind — Sea and river wave sounds**:
  https://opengameart.org/content/sea-and-river-wave-sounds . The creator assigns
  CC0 and describes recording waves on the Vistula river. We use that river
  recording as a restrained waterside bed; it is not relabeled as ocean field
  recording.

CC0 legal terms: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Commercial
use, modification, incorporation and redistribution are permitted. We retain
credit and provenance voluntarily for traceability. No subscription, login,
attribution condition or share-alike term was present in these CC0 grants.

The original publication HTML and common CC0 legal text are retained under
`LicenseEvidence/`; their hashes appear in `license-evidence.json`. Their
purpose is the contemporaneous license record, not executable game content.

`Legacy/` contains this project's pre-remaster JavaScript source, not a
third-party audio grant. Its inclusion here does not relicense that code.
